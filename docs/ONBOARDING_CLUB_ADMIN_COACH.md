# Club Admin & Coach onboarding

Short guide for getting a club running and coaching athletes in **Every Mile Counts**.

---

## Roles at a glance

| Role | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **Club admin** | Create and run the club: invites, roster, coach assignments |
| **Coach** | Review athletes, assign training plans and workouts |
| **Head coach** | Club admin who also coaches (recommended for small clubs) |

- Club admins manage the club; coaches work with **assigned** athletes only.
- Coaches usually also train as athletes (own dashboard, Strava, events).
- Club-only admins (no athlete/coach role) manage the club but do not get the athlete training screens.

---

## Club admin — first setup

1. **Register** as Club (with invitation code if required).
2. **Complete profile** — add your name and **club name**. That creates the club.
3. Answer the **head coach** prompt:
   - **Yes** — you become coach for the club and can accept athletes right away.
   - **No** — add at least one coach before athletes can join (club stays *pending coach* until then).
4. Open **Clubs** → your club.
5. Create **QR / invite codes** for athletes (and coaches if needed) and share the join link.
6. On **Requests**, approve join requests and **assign coaches** to athletes.

### Day to day

- Approve or decline join requests
- Assign / reassign coaches
- Refresh or disable invite QR codes
- Update club details and announcements
- Remind athletes to connect **Strava** and share with coach (needed for activity reviews)

### Limits to know

- A user can be in up to **3 clubs**
- An athlete can have up to **3 coaches**
- Up to **20 active** club invite codes
- Club admins cannot join other clubs as a normal member, and cannot leave their own club via Leave
- Membership must be active for most features

---

## Coach — first setup

1. **Register** as Coach, **or** join via a coach invite / QR from the club admin.
2. **Complete your profile** (name, date of birth).
3. Wait until the club admin **assigns** you to athletes (or an athlete adds you if you share a club).
4. Open **Coaching** — your athletes and review inbox appear there.
5. Optional: connect **Strava** for your own training.

### Day to day

| Task | Where |
|------|--------|
| See athletes & review requests | **Coaching** |
| Review an activity | Athlete → activity → write review |
| Build / assign training plans | **Plans** / Coaching → Training |
| One-off assigned sessions | Coaching → new activity / assign |
| Athlete groups | Coaching → Groups |
| Your own runs & events | Dashboard, Activities, Events |

### Limits to know

- You only see athletes you are **assigned** to
- Strava activities need the athlete to allow coach visibility for reviews
- You can edit programs you own; stick to assigned athletes

## How admin and coach work together

```
Club admin creates club
        ↓
Head coach? ──yes──→ Admin also coaches members
        │
        no → Admin adds coach account(s)
        ↓
Invite athletes (QR / link / email)
        ↓
Approve joins → Assign coach to each athlete
        ↓
Coach reviews activities & assigns training
```

**Tip:** For a small club, turn on **head coach** so one person can admin and coach. For larger clubs, keep admin and coach as separate accounts — club admins cannot also sit as a normal club coach on another path; use a dedicated coach login instead.

---

## Quick checklist

**Club admin**
- [ ] Club created and profile complete  
- [ ] Head coach set **or** at least one coach added  
- [ ] Athlete invite QR/link shared  
- [ ] Join requests approved  
- [ ] Each athlete has a coach assigned  

**Coach**
- [ ] Profile complete  
- [ ] Visible under Coaching with assigned athletes  
- [ ] Review inbox checked  
- [ ] First plan or workout assigned (optional)  
- [ ] Athletes reminded to connect Strava + coach share  

---

Questions about access or membership? Use **Profile** and **Membership**, or ask your platform admin for invite codes and plan status.
